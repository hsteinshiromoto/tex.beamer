# 2026-09-22 — Modernize the header, the footer, and the typefaces

## What changed

### Typefaces

The body now uses Helvetica. `src/conf/settings.tex` loads it with `\IfFontExistsTF`. When
Helvetica is absent, the template falls back to TeX Gyre Heros, the previous body font. TeX
Gyre Heros is URW Nimbus Sans and matches the metrics of Helvetica, so the fallback keeps the
line breaks and the page count.

The header, the footer, and the footnotes now use Jost\*, vendored in `src/fonts/`. The
template loads it as `\headerfont` through `\newfontfamily` with an explicit `Path`.

The first request asked for Futura. Three findings ruled it out:

1. TeX Live ships no Futura package. A search of the whole `texmf` tree for `futura`, `jost`,
   and `renner` returned nothing. There is no dependency to declare.
2. Futura is macOS only and proprietary. The CI job compiles in an Ubuntu container, which
   has no Futura.
3. Futura Medium, the regular weight, carries `fsType = 0x0006`. Bit 1 of that value is the
   Restricted License embedding bit. This repository publishes `main.pdf` as a release asset
   under a Zenodo DOI, so the PDF is distributed.

Jost\* is a Futura revival under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 with no Reserved Font Name
clause. The repository can carry it. Its `fsType` is `0x0000`, so the PDF can embed it.

### Header

The `smoothbars` gradient is gone. A new `headline` template draws a flat bar in `ggrey900`.
`\insertnavigation` still draws the section names and the mini frames. The section names use
`\allcaps` from `conf/commands.tex`. The active section is white and the rest are grey. The
mini frames are smaller, at 1.6 mm.

The bar uses `ht=2.3ex` and `dp=2.7ex`. The first attempt used `ht=3ex` and `dp=2ex`, which
pushed the text and the dots against the bottom edge. A pixel probe of the 62 px bar showed
19 px of space above the ink and 1 px below it. The new values center the ink, at 11 px above
and 10 px below. The sum of `ht` and `dp` stays at `5ex`, so the bar height and `\headheight`
do not change. The title page and the section pages therefore keep their blue band.

### Footer

The footer is a dark band in `ggrey900`, the same color as the header bar. It holds the email
on the left, the short title in the center, and the slide number out of the total on the
right. The text is `ggrey400` and the current slide number is `gblue300`. Both are light
colors, because the band is dark.

The progress bar sits directly on top of the band and forms its top edge. The filled part is
`gblue500` and the track is `ggrey700`. Pixel probes confirm the geometry. Page 4 of 13 gives
30.6 percent filled against an expected 30.8 percent. Page 13 of 13 gives 99.7 percent. The
row between the bar and the band measures `(67,67,67)`, which is an antialiasing blend of the
two. There is no seam.

An earlier version put the bar on the bottom edge of the page below a hairline rule, on a
white footer. The current version replaced it.

## Problems found and fixed

1. The progress bar rendered as a one pixel sliver. Its baseline fell about 5 px below the
   paper edge, so most of the rule was cut. A trailing `\vskip1.5pt` moved it inside the page.
   Pixel probes confirm the fix: page 4 of 13 gives 489 blue pixels out of 1600, which is
   30.6 percent against an expected 30.8 percent. Page 13 of 13 gives 99.8 percent.
2. `\allcaps{\insertshorttitle}` letterspaced the footer title but did not uppercase it.
   `\MakeTextUppercase` does not reach inside `\insertshorttitle`. The result read as
   "X e L a T e X  T e m p l a t e". The footer title is now plain.
3. The header content sat against the bottom of the bar. See the Header section above.
4. `\nointerlineskip` between the progress bar and the footer band raised
   "You can't use `\prevdepth' in horizontal mode". `\nointerlineskip` assigns
   `\prevdepth`, which needs vertical mode, and the `\leavevmode` above it had already
   entered horizontal mode. TeX inserts `\par` for a vertical command in horizontal mode,
   which is why the neighboring `\vskip` worked, but `\prevdepth` is an internal quantity
   and gets no such treatment. The fix wraps both rows in a `\vbox`. Note that the build
   hangs rather than fails here, because the Makefile does not set an interaction mode and
   XeLaTeX waits at the error prompt. Rerun with `-interaction=nonstopmode` to see the error.

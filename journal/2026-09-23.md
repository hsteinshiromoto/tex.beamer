# 2026-09-23 - Center the frame title in its band

## What changed

`src/conf/settings.tex` replaces the stock `frametitle` template with its own. The title now
sits in the vertical center of the blue band.

## Why the stock template put it low

The old line was:

```tex
\setbeamertemplate{frametitle}[default][colsep=-4bp,rounded=false,shadow=false]
```

The stock template in `beamerouterthemedefault.sty` pulls the title up with `\vbox{}\vskip-1ex`
at the top. It cancels the bottom padding with `\vskip-.3cm` at the bottom. That second skip
runs only inside an `\if@tempswa\else` branch, which means it runs only when the caller gives
no optional argument. This template gives one, so the bottom skip never ran. The band kept its
full bottom padding while the top skip still pulled the title up.

Measured on a 107 px band: 39 px above the ink and 15 px below it.

## The fix

The new template drops both vertical skips and uses a symmetric `sep`. The struts around
`\insertframetitle` keep the line box the same height whether or not the title has a
descender, so the result does not depend on the words in the title.

## Problem found and fixed

`sep` pads all four sides of a `beamercolorbox`. Tightening it to hold the band near its old
height therefore also pulled the title left, from x=31 px to x=14 px. `leftskip=0.15cm` tops
the left padding back up to the 0.3cm that the stock template uses.

## Verification

- `make` runs clean. Zero errors, zero overfull or underfull boxes.
- Page 4, title "The First Frame: equation", which has a descender: 29 px above, 27 px below.
- Page 13, title "A Title", which has no descender: 29 px above, 38 px below. The line box is
  centered. The extra space below is the unused descender room.
- The title starts at x=31 px, the same as before the change.
- The band measures 109 px against 107 px before the change.
